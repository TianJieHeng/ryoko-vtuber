# Ryoko VTuber — Open Design Decisions

**Phase:** 1  
**Status:** Tracked proposals; none may be silently treated as measured source geometry

| Topic | Working proposal | Validation needed |
|---|---|---|
| Hidden scalp and tie construction | Use a compact gathered knot/base beneath the bun-loop and two hair sticks | Inspect in blockout from rear/top views |
| Hair-stick hanging ornaments | Follow the accessory sheet's two short chain/drop elements on the lower stick | Check visibility, collisions, and silhouette against neutral/back masters |
| Mouth interior | Build a restrained adult teeth arcade, tongue, and dark oral cavity | Test jaw extremes, lip sealing, and side views |
| Hidden blouse construction | Keep the blouse tucked with a simple buttoned lower section | Check blazer/trouser intersections in body poses |
| Absolute heel and accessory dimensions | Derive from the approved 1.83 m overall convention and reference proportions | Check standing balance and VRM scale |
| Rear hair segmentation | Use a rigid gathered base plus a small number of deformable ponytail/nape groups | Test idle, head turns, and scolding gestures |
| Material palette | Sample/calibrate clean albedo in Blender rather than copying painted highlights and shadows | Compare under neutral Cycles lighting in Phase 3 |
| Illustrated effects | Defer tears, blush, sweat, sparkle eyes, graphic marks, and chibi effects to a separately approved effects pass | Confirm desired scope before Phase 7 |
| Props | Exclude folders, mugs, papers, and business cards from the base avatar unless explicitly requested | Scope props with the performances that need them |
| Performance budget | Begin with the Phase 1 high-detail budget and reduce only where measured or visible testing requires it | User-side VSeeFace/runtime testing |
| AI-control host | Keep action/state IDs runtime-neutral until the control bridge is selected | Validate selection, blending, interruption, and neutral recovery |

No open item authorizes an unrelated redesign. Where the references disagree, the neutral face and front/back masters remain authoritative.
