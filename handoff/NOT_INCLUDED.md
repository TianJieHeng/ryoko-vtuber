# What This Reference Pack Does Not Include

This is a visual modeling and acting reference pack. Actual file acceptance and publication are reported in the [asset inventory](../metadata/asset_index.json) and [publication status](../metadata/publication_status.json); the 273 delivered primary sources and their derivatives are not a working avatar.

## Missing control references

- **CTL-jawLeft:** attempts did not reliably isolate lateral jaw movement. No accepted jawLeft reference is delivered.
- **CTL-lowerLipDepress:** attempts did not reliably isolate lower-lip depression and the intended lower-teeth exposure. No accepted lowerLipDepress reference is delivered.
- The 22 accepted generic control references are not a complete or certified ARKit 52-shape set. The jawRight guide has three comparison panels, not nine temporal poses. Other reference controls may include natural coupled movements; use their review notes.

## No implemented model or rig

The pack does not include a 3D mesh, measured orthographic model, retopology, UV unwrap, model-fitted texture atlas, skin weights, skeleton, facial shape keys, calibrated control weights, corrective shapes, physics, hair simulation, `.blend` project, VRM export or other tested runtime model.

Hidden anatomy, mouth construction and hair/accessory attachment details remain modeling decisions. Reference art is not evidence that a proposed surface exists or deforms correctly in three dimensions.

## No finished animation playback

The nine-key-pose sheets and losslessly extracted panels are visual guides. They are not pixel-registered frames, generated in-betweens, animation curves, rigged clips or tested seamless loops. Panel sizes and framing can vary. Proposed timestamps are acting guidance, not measured playback.

The 40 body references are reusable performances paired with facial expressions in the catalogue. They are not 200 individually synchronized, combined full-body animations. Cropped panels and peak copies do not add new primary expressions or source sheets.

## No ready effect layers or fitted textures

Blush, tears, sweat, sparkle eyes, graphic marks and other accents may appear within the illustrations. They are not separately supplied, registered UV overlays or implemented material effects. Sheet backgrounds, annotations, dividers, lettering and comic/chibi elements must not silently become avatar textures.

Native dimensions are recorded in metadata. No native 2K/4K quality, calibrated photometric color, uniform pixel registration or animation-ready transparency is implied.

## No runtime compatibility certification

The full-character wall-projection use case is confirmed, but the target runtime, tracking route and export version remain unspecified. No Blender deformation, app import, lip-sync/tracking, playback, hardware performance or physical projection test has been performed by this reference-art workflow.

No direct task or communication has been sent to Kimi as part of publishing these files. The user can supply this handoff to their chosen builder. Model construction, controls, animation, export and application testing remain implementation work.
