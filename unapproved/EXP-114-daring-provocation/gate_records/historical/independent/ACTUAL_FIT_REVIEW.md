# EXP-114: independent actual static fit review

**Decision: PASS for the frozen seven-state fixed-head facial interpretation, with retained amber limitations.** This is a combined actual-pixel expressive and material pass, not only a control or headless pass. It is not approval of an animated performance, transitions, a new registered profile, publication, or a further engine run.

## Expressive judgment

I inspected the actual native1x face pixels for every state, each original actual0.49 bust PNG, the original source panels and source-to-native face pairs, and enlarged diagnostic mouth/eye crops. The enlarged crops are evidence layouts, not additional renders. Frontal source KP6, rather than canted KP5, is the decisive comparison.

At actual bust scale, KP6 retains a restrained cocky, forward challenge. The narrowed unequal amber openings keep eye contact; the slightly unequal brow line and conspicuously higher screen-right mouth corner oppose an otherwise composed face. The exposed crooked tooth band changes that attention into a deliberate teasing dare. It does not read as a bright, symmetrical welcome. This judgment comes from the combined pixels, not the label or the fact that the channels have nonzero values.

The source's inner-brow opposition and peak force are stronger. The fixed canonical brow and lower-lid shapes soften that force, particularly at0.49 scale. This is a lower-intensity interpretation, not an exact recreation of the drawing's central brow compression. That limitation remains amber; it is not erased by the PASS. KP5 also omits the real source head cant. No head, neck or gaze substitute was used, and I do not claim that KP5's whole original pose is reproduced. The frontal KP6 still independently carries the intended facial category without that cant.

## Seven-state observations

- Neutral start: canonical direct amber gaze and sealed lips. It is visually and byte-for-byte the recovered neutral.
- KP3: a restrained sealed smile lifts toward screen-right while ordinary lids narrow. This is a preparation, not claimed as a distinctive final daring peak by itself.
- KP4: the crooked smile parts into a very thin enamel sliver. At native1x it remains bounded by lips; at actual0.49 it reads as a fine bright parting line rather than a broad grin. It is a defensible shallow transition anchor, not a tooth display peak.
- KP5: a broader continuous upper-enamel grin with a stronger screen-right corner and unequal narrowed lids. It carries the facial peak while the head remains fixed. The original source cant is excluded, explicitly.
- KP6: the tooth-bearing challenge persists frontally, with the screen-left eye now the more narrowed of the pair. Visible amber and attentive direct contact survive at actual bust scale. This is the decisive non-wink, non-welcome state.
- KP7: the mouth reseals and mostly releases while the eyes retain a little narrowed attention. No residual enamel or cavity leak is visible.
- Recovered neutral: exact PNG bytes and decoded RGBA match start in both native and bust views; direct owner and face-state snapshots also match exactly.

## Material and joins

The native mouth crops show a continuous existing upper-enamel band at KP5/6. The darker triangular ends taper into the lip corners; the warm upper/lower lip borders remain attached. I do not observe a detached tooth strip, exposed root slab, new central gap, lower-row substitution, warm wedge through the enamel, or a facial plate hiding the opening. The shallow KP4 enamel is thin and visibly segmented at enlargement but is not a broken material at the actual native scale. Existing more detailed enamel and fuller canonical lips differ from the source's flatter painted band.

The source114 panels do not demand the opposed segmented lower dental row seen in148. These actual pixels support the bounded upper-enamel trial; they do not establish a clench capability. The known maximum-open/spread/corner stress wedge remains unfixed. This PASS covers only the frozen openings0/.12/.34/.30 and associated exact frozen poses, never an extrapolated maximum range.

All actual eyes retain amber, sclera and separate direct iris owners. The canonical lash/hair crossing roughness and small inherited edge fragments remain visible in native detail, including neutral. They were neither hidden nor repaired. At these bounded states I do not observe a new floating anatomical piece, disappearing pupil, or broken head/body connection. Existing contour/crease limitations are retained rather than used alone to reject the combined facial fit.
