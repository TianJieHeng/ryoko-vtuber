### Eyes/brows: plausible body motion, with a terminal residual

The source-relative onset and recovery are modest and the current controls implement them cleanly. Both eyes remain open and become slightly heavier. At KP5/6 the canonical brows lower and tilt, but the visible inner contours remain smooth and comparatively unsqueezed. At native face and bust scales the result reads as mild lowered sternness rather than the source's concentrated inward frustration.
